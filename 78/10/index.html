Konsep yang harus diketahui: Untuk mencari nilai $x^3+y^3$, kita tidak perlu mencari nilai $x$ dan $y$ satu per satu. Kita bisa menggunakan rumus identitas aljabar. Ada dua rumus penting yang perlu kita ingat:
1. $(x+y)^2 = x^2 + 2xy + y^2$ (Rumus kuadrat)
2. $x^3+y^3 = (x+y)(x^2 - xy + y^2)$ (Rumus pemfaktoran pangkat tiga)

Langkah 1: Mencari nilai $xy$. Dari rumus pertama, mari kita masukkan angka yang sudah kita ketahui dari soal: $x+y=5$ dan $x^2+y^2=13$. $$\begin{aligned} (x+y)^2 &= (x^2+y^2) + 2xy \\ (5)^2 &= 13 + 2xy \\ 25 &= 13 + 2xy \end{aligned}$$ Pindahkan 13 ke ruas kiri (menjadi pengurangan): $25 - 13 = 2xy \implies 12 = 2xy \implies xy = 6$.

Langkah 2: Menghitung $x^3+y^3$. Gunakan rumus kedua dan masukkan semua angka yang kita punya: $$\begin{aligned} x^3+y^3 &= (x+y) \times (x^2 + y^2 - xy) \\ x^3+y^3 &= 5 \times (13 - 6) \\ x^3+y^3 &= 5 \times 7 = 35 \end{aligned}$$

EN:

Concepts to know: To find the value of $x^3+y^3$, we do not need to find the exact values of $x$ and $y$ individually. We can use algebraic identities. There are two important formulas to remember:
1. $(x+y)^2 = x^2 + 2xy + y^2$ (Perfect square formula)
2. $x^3+y^3 = (x+y)(x^2 - xy + y^2)$ (Sum of cubes factorization)

Step 1: Find the value of $xy$. From the first formula, let's plug in the numbers we know: $x+y=5$ and $x^2+y^2=13$. $$\begin{aligned} (x+y)^2 &= (x^2+y^2) + 2xy \\ (5)^2 &= 13 + 2xy \\ 25 &= 13 + 2xy \end{aligned}$$ Move 13 to the left side (it becomes subtraction): $25 - 13 = 2xy \implies 12 = 2xy \implies xy = 6$.

Step 2: Calculate $x^3+y^3$. Use the second formula and insert all the numbers we have: $$\begin{aligned} x^3+y^3 &= (x+y) \times (x^2 + y^2 - xy) \\ x^3+y^3 &= 5 \times (13 - 6) \\ x^3+y^3 &= 5 \times 7 = 35 \end{aligned}$$

Answer: 35

Langkah 2: Mencari $a+b$ dan $ab$. Menggunakan Teorema Vieta:
• $a + b = -\frac{(-7)}{1} = 7$
• $a \times b = \frac{10}{1} = 10$

Langkah 3: Menjawab Pertanyaan. Pertanyaannya meminta nilai dari $\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b}$. Samakan penyebutnya menjadi $ab$: $$ \frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} = \frac{b}{ab} + \frac{a}{ab} = \frac{a+b}{ab} $$ Langsung masukkan angkanya: $\frac{a+b}{ab} = \frac{7}{10}$.

EN:

Concepts to know: The general form of a quadratic equation is $Ax^2 + Bx + C = 0$. According to Vieta's Formulas, if a quadratic equation has roots $a$ and $b$, the following relationships apply: Sum of roots $a + b = -\frac{B}{A}$ and Product of roots $a \times b = \frac{C}{A}$.

Step 1: Identify $A, B$, and $C$. From the equation $x^2 - 7x + 10 = 0$, $A = 1$, $B = -7$, and $C = 10$.

Step 2: Find $a+b$ and $ab$. Using Vieta's formulas:
• $a + b = -\frac{(-7)}{1} = 7$
• $a \times b = \frac{10}{1} = 10$

Step 3: Answer the Question. The question asks for $\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b}$. To add fractions, we need a common denominator ($ab$): $$ \frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} = \frac{b}{ab} + \frac{a}{ab} = \frac{a+b}{ab} $$ Since we know $a+b = 7$ and $ab = 10$, we simply substitute the numbers: $\frac{a+b}{ab} = \frac{7}{10}$.

Answer: 7/10 (or 0.7)

Konsep yang harus diketahui: Menyamakan penyebut sampai 100 sangat mustahil. Namun, ada trik aljabar khusus yang disebut pecahan parsial (deret teleskopik). Perhatikan pola ini: $\frac{1}{2 \times 3} \text{ sama saja dengan } \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{3}$. Secara umum, rumusnya adalah: $\frac{1}{n \times (n+1)} = \frac{1}{n} - \frac{1}{n+1}$

Langkah Penyelesaian: Kita ubah setiap suku di dalam soal menggunakan trik di atas. Mari kita jumlahkan semuanya memanjang ke samping: $$ \left( \frac{1}{1} - \frac{1}{2} \right) + \left( \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{3} \right) + \dots + \left( \frac{1}{99} - \frac{1}{100} \right) $$

Perhatikan keajaibannya! Terdapat angka pengurang $-1/2$ yang langsung bertemu dengan penambah $+1/2$. Mereka saling menghilangkan. Ini terjadi terus-menerus! Yang tersisa hanyalah angka paling depan dan angka paling belakang: $$ \frac{1}{1} - \frac{1}{100} = 1 - 0.01 = \frac{100}{100} - \frac{1}{100} = \frac{99}{100} $$

EN:

Concepts to know: Adding so many fractions manually is impossible. However, there is a special algebraic trick called partial fractions (or telescoping series). Notice this pattern: $\frac{1}{2 \times 3} \text{ is exactly the same as } \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{3}$. In general, the formula is: $\frac{1}{n \times (n+1)} = \frac{1}{n} - \frac{1}{n+1}$

Step-by-step Solution: We change every single term in the question using the trick above. Now, let's add everything horizontally: $$ \left( \frac{1}{1} - \frac{1}{2} \right) + \left( \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{3} \right) + \dots + \left( \frac{1}{99} - \frac{1}{100} \right) $$

Notice the magic! The subtracted $-1/2$ meets the added $+1/2$ and they cancel each other out to zero. This domino effect happens continuously up to $1/99$. The only numbers left are the very first and the very last: $$ \frac{1}{1} - \frac{1}{100} = 1 - 0.01 = \frac{100}{100} - \frac{1}{100} = \frac{99}{100} $$

Answer: 99/100

Konsep yang harus diketahui: Persamaan ini terlihat rumit karena pangkatnya. Trik rahasianya adalah "Permisalan" (Substitusi). Ingat sifat pangkat: $4^x = (2^2)^x = 2^{2x} = (2^x)^2$.

Langkah 1: Melakukan Permisalan. Ubah persamaan awal menjadi: $(2^x)^2 - 3(2^x) + 2 = 0$. Misalkan $y = 2^x$. Maka persamaannya berubah menjadi persamaan kuadrat biasa: $y^2 - 3y + 2 = 0$.

Langkah 2: Memfaktorkan Persamaan. Kita cari dua angka yang jika dikalikan hasilnya $+2$ dan jika ditambahkan hasilnya $-3$. Yaitu $-1$ dan $-2$. Maka bentuk pemfaktorannya $(y - 1)(y - 2) = 0$. Solusinya adalah $y = 1$ atau $y = 2$.

Langkah 3: Mengembalikan Permisalan. Kembalikan $y$ menjadi $2^x$.
• Kasus 1: $y = 1 \implies 2^x = 1$. Angka dipangkatkan $0$ hasilnya adalah $1$. Jadi, $x = 0$.
• Kasus 2: $y = 2 \implies 2^x = 2$. Dua pangkat $1$ hasilnya dua. Jadi, $x = 1$.

EN:

Concepts to know: This equation looks complicated because of the power $x$. The secret trick is "Substitution". Remember exponent rules: $4^x = (2^2)^x = 2^{2x} = (2^x)^2$. So, we can change the number 4 into a power of 2.

Step 1: Make a Substitution. Change the equation to: $(2^x)^2 - 3(2^x) + 2 = 0$. To make it easier to read, let's replace $2^x$ with $y$. So let: $y = 2^x$. The equation becomes a standard quadratic equation: $y^2 - 3y + 2 = 0$.

Step 2: Factor the Equation. We look for two numbers that multiply to $+2$ and add up to $-3$. Those numbers are $-1$ and $-2$. Thus, $(y - 1)(y - 2) = 0$. The solutions for $y$ are $y = 1$ or $y = 2$.

Step 3: Revert the Substitution. Remember, we need $x$, not $y$. Revert $y$ back to $2^x$.
• Case 1: $y = 1 \implies 2^x = 1$. Any non-zero number to the power of 0 is 1. Thus, $x = 0$.
• Case 2: $y = 2 \implies 2^x = 2$. Two to the power of 1 is 2. Thus, $x = 1$.

Answer: x = 0 or x = 1

EN:

Concepts to know: In math olympiads, finding minimum values without calculus usually involves the AM-GM Inequality (Arithmetic Mean - Geometric Mean). The theorem states: For two positive numbers $A$ and $B$, their arithmetic mean is always greater than or equal to their geometric mean. Formula: $\frac{A + B}{2} \geq \sqrt{A \times B}$.

Step 1: Set $A$ and $B$. Let the first term $A = x$, and the second term $B = \frac{9}{x}$.

Step 2: Apply the AM-GM formula. $$ \frac{x + \frac{9}{x}}{2} \geq \sqrt{x \times \frac{9}{x}} $$ Look inside the square root! The $x$ cancels out: $x \times \frac{9}{x} = 9$. $$ \frac{x + \frac{9}{x}}{2} \geq \sqrt{9} \implies \frac{x + \frac{9}{x}}{2} \geq 3 $$

Step 3: Find the minimum value. Move the 2 to the right side (multiply): $$ x + \frac{9}{x} \geq 3 \times 2 \implies x + \frac{9}{x} \geq 6 $$ This means the calculation will never be less than 6. The minimum possible value is exactly 6.

The function returns to the original starting number!

Konsep yang harus diketahui: Pecahan dengan akar di bagian penyebut harus dirasionalkan dengan cara mengalikan sekawannya. Ingat rumus aljabar: $(A + B)(A - B) = A^2 - B^2$.

Langkah 1: Merasionalkan satu suku sebagai contoh. Suku $\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}+\sqrt{2}}$ kita kalikan dengan sekawannya yaitu $\sqrt{3}-\sqrt{2}$: $$ \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}+\sqrt{2}} \times \frac{\sqrt{3}-\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{3}-\sqrt{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}-\sqrt{2}}{3 - 2} = \sqrt{3} - \sqrt{2} $$ Sifat ajaib ini berlaku persis untuk semua pecahannya! Menjadi deret: $(\sqrt{2} - 1) + (\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{2}) + \dots + (\sqrt{100} - \sqrt{99})$.

Langkah 2: Menjumlahkan semuanya. Perhatikan ada efek domino saling coret. Terdapat $+\sqrt{2}$ yang bertemu $-\sqrt{2}$ sehingga saling menghilangkan. Ini berlanjut terus. Hampir semua angka tercoret, kecuali angka negatif terkecil di depan ($-1$) dan angka positif terbesar di akhir ($+\sqrt{100}$).

Langkah 3: Hitung sisa akhir. Sisa hitungannya adalah: $\sqrt{100} - 1 = 10 - 1 = 9$.

EN:

Concepts to know: Having square roots in the denominator is considered "unsimplified". We fix this by "Rationalizing the Denominator" (multiplying by the conjugate). The conjugate of a plus is a minus. Rule: $(A+B)(A-B) = A^2-B^2$.

Step 1: Rationalize one term as an example. Take the second term: $\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}+\sqrt{2}}$. Multiply by its conjugate $\sqrt{3}-\sqrt{2}$. $$ \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}+\sqrt{2}} \times \frac{\sqrt{3}-\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{3}-\sqrt{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}-\sqrt{2}}{(\sqrt{3})^2 - (\sqrt{2})^2} = \sqrt{3} - \sqrt{2} $$ This magic happens to all terms! Generating the series: $(\sqrt{2} - 1) + (\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{2}) + \dots + (\sqrt{100} - \sqrt{99})$.

Step 2: Add them all together. Notice the domino effect. The $+\sqrt{2}$ and $-\sqrt{2}$ cancel out to zero. So does $\sqrt{3}$, and so on. The only survivors are the smallest negative number at the front ($-1$) and the largest positive number at the end ($+\sqrt{100}$).

Step 3: Final Calculation. $\sqrt{100} - 1 = 10 - 1 = 9$.

Answer: 9
